Patch

Paint out dust, specks and hairs: click on a speck, drag along a hair. Each stroke is filled with a copy of the picture from close by, matched in color and brightness to where it goes, so the grain of the paper and the paint carries on and nothing is blurred; a long stroke is filled in stretches, each copied from a place of its own. Hover over a patch to see where its copies came from (yellow); drag one somewhere better if the patch shows. Option-click a patch to take it out.

Dust
select

Hairs, then specks and lint, that stand out from the grain round them, strongest first, circled in the picture. Paint, too, can stand out that much: click a picture to leave it out, double-click to see it where it is. A hair is a line thinner than a brush paints with the same paint either side; round marks over 20 px across start left out, as most are paint. A hair's faint tail may be left: drag along it.

Export

A preset is a list of files to make from one result — say a full-size TIFF, a 300 dpi PNG, a PNG under 10 MB and a JPEG — each with a file name of your own. The picture is rendered once and every file is made from it. TIFFs are always lossless.

Batch

A folder with many artworks in it: every set is stitched, straightened and cropped by the rules of the Straighten page, colored as chosen, and saved with a preset — unattended. A set that goes wrong is reported and the rest carry on; one that finishes with something worth a look is marked review, and opens in the editor exactly as the batch left it.
